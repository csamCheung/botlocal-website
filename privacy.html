Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-18

1. Who we are

BotSquirrel ("we", "us") provides an AI customer-service assistant SaaS for small businesses. Channels available today are WhatsApp and website live chat.

BotSquirrel is a trading name of BotSquirrel Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17364331), with its registered office at 71–75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London, WC2H 9JQ, United Kingdom.

2. Two roles in which we handle data

(a) As a data controller for the account data you (our business customer) use to sign up and manage your account — e.g. your name, business name, email, WhatsApp number and billing details.
(b) As a data processor for the end-customer conversation data we process on your behalf when your customers chat with your bot. That data belongs to you; we process it only on your instructions and to provide the service.

3. Data we collect / process

When a customer chats with a bot via WhatsApp or website live chat, we process: message content, phone number or account identifier, and any contact details the customer voluntarily provides (e.g. name, email). For business accounts, we process the data needed for login, contact and billing.

4. How data is used

Data is used only to: answer enquiries, follow up on contact requests, operate and improve the service, and handle billing. We never sell data to unrelated third parties, and we never use your data to train other companies' models.

5. Third-party services

To provide the service we use trusted providers:

· Amazon Web Services (London, eu-west-2) — hosting, database and file storage for the account, billing and dashboard systems;
· OpenAI (United States) — generating AI replies and embeddings from your approved business information;
· Meta Platforms — delivering WhatsApp messages via the WhatsApp Business Cloud API;
· Paddle — subscription payments (once live) as Merchant of Record;
· Railway — hosting for the messaging engine.

Each provider has its own privacy policy. Where these providers process your end customers' conversation data on our behalf, they act as our sub-processors — see the Data Processing Addendum for the full list, their locations, and the processor terms we operate under.

6. Retention & deletion

We keep personal data only for as long as it is needed to provide the service, and delete it once it is not:

· Conversation history and enquiry records — kept while the business customer's account is active. When an account closes, we delete or anonymise the personal data it holds within 90 days;
· Account and billing records — kept 6 years after the account closes, as UK tax law requires;
· Encrypted backups — overwritten on a rolling cycle, so data removed from our live systems can remain in backups for up to 30 days before it is gone entirely.

You may ask us to delete your conversation history and contact details at any time, using the contact details below. We will respond within one month, as the UK GDPR requires. We may keep data longer only where the law requires it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you are, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, including your rights under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. To exercise these rights, contact us.

You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint. We'd appreciate the chance to put things right first, so please come to us before you do — but you don't have to.

8. Cookies

This marketing site sets no cookies at all. The app at app.botsquirrel.com sets strictly-necessary authentication cookies only — the sign-in session cookies authjs.session-token and, for our own platform staff, pa_session. We use no advertising or analytics cookies, so there is no consent banner to click through.
